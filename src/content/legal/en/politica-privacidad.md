## 2. What data this policy applies to

Belrion processes two different types of personal data, with different roles under the GDPR:

### 2.1 Data of the Belrion user account (you)
When you register and use Belrion, your email and password are processed (the latter encrypted, managed by Supabase Auth) to allow you to securely sign in. Here, **Xavier Rosselló Díaz acts as the data controller**.

### 2.2 Data of the clients you enter into the application
Belrion lets you save data about your own clients (company name, contact people, phone, email, address, contracted products, follow-up notes). **For this data, you are the data controller** (you decide which data about your clients you enter and for what purpose), and **Xavier Rosselló Díaz acts solely as the data processor**, i.e., as the technical provider that hosts and processes that data on your behalf, without deciding its purpose or using it for his own ends.

> In practice: if you publish or share Belrion with other people, each of them will be responsible for the data of their own clients that they enter into the app, and must have a legitimate basis to process it (for example, an existing business relationship with those clients).

## 3. Purpose of processing

- Managing your access and authentication in the application.
- Allowing you to organize your client portfolio, sales visits, and associated follow-up.
- Sending you notifications/reminders for visits that you schedule yourself.
- Maintaining the security and proper functioning of the service.

The data entered is not used for our own advertising purposes, nor shared with third parties other than the technology providers necessary to provide the service (see section 5).

## 4. Legal basis

- **Your account data:** performance of the contract/terms of use you accept when registering.
- **Your clients' data:** the legal basis is yours as the controller of that data (typically, the contractual or pre-contractual relationship with your own clients, or their consent, depending on the case).

As the owner of your own data (your account), you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object, and request portability of your data by writing to **soportebelrion@gmail.com**. You also have the right to file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) if you believe your rights have not been properly addressed ([www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)).

If you are a person whose data has been entered by a Belrion user (for example, you are the contact for a client company registered in a salesperson's app), you should direct your request directly to that person or company, as they act as the controller of your data.

## 8. Security measures

Belrion applies reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data, including:

- Encryption of passwords and communications (HTTPS/TLS).
- Row Level Security (RLS) in the database, which prevents one user from accessing another's data.
- Restricted access to the service's configuration keys and secrets.
